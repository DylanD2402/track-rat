Track editor

Draws a session's laps from its log, with the track's start/finish, splits and corners on top. Move the line, flip its direction, add or remove splits, name the corners, or make a new layout. Saving needs the upload code and restarts Track Rat. Then re-time to apply the lines to saved sessions.

Re-time

Track Rat times sessions again from their logs with the lines it has now, and rewrites their lap times and the PB. It carries on in the background.
